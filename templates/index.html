{# ---------------------------------------------------------- FEATURE CARDS #}

What JobShield AI does

Two independent checks, shown side by side and never blended into a single verdict.

  • AI-powered text analysis

    A machine-learning model trained on thousands of real postings reads the wording and estimates whether it resembles known fraudulent adverts.

  • Explainable warning signs

    A transparent rule engine flags risky patterns — upfront fees, sensitive-data requests, urgent pressure — and shows the exact words that triggered each one.

  • Analysis history

    Every analysis you run is saved to your own private list, so you can compare postings and revisit what you found later.

  • Recruitment scam awareness

    Each warning sign comes with a plain-language explanation and a practical next step, so you learn to recognise the pattern yourself.

How it works

Three steps, one page, no account needed.

  1. 1

    Submit a job posting

    Paste the title and description. Add the company name and location if the advert includes them.

  2. 2

    Analyze its content

    The text is turned into numbers the model can read, the model makes an estimate, and the rule engine looks for risky wording.

  3. 3

    Review the prediction and warning signs

    You get the model's estimate and the warning signs in two separate panels, plus the evidence behind each one.

{# --------------------------------------------------------- RESPONSIBLE USE #}

Use this responsibly

JobShield AI is a decision-support tool, not a judge. A high estimate is not proof of fraud, and a low estimate is not proof that an employer is genuine. The tool has no way to check company registrations, business records or link reputations, because it is not connected to any such service.

Always confirm an opportunity through independent, official sources — the company's own careers page, a phone number you looked up yourself, or a person you trust who works there. Never pay money to obtain a job.
